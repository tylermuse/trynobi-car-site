Most dealer websites still work the same way they did a decade ago. A shopper lands on the site, scrolls through filters, maybe fills out a lead form, and leaves. The dealership gets a name and an email address with zero context on what that person actually wants.

Nobi replaces that dead-end experience with a conversation. It's an [AI shopping assistant](/blog/ai-shopping-assistant-auto-dealers) that sits on your dealer website and lets shoppers search your inventory, ask questions about specific vehicles, and get real answers, all in natural language. No dropdowns. No waiting for a callback. No form fills that go into a black hole.

This article breaks down exactly how Nobi works, from the shopper's first question to the moment a qualified lead hits your sales team's desk.

## What Nobi Does

Nobi is an AI-powered vehicle search and shopping assistant built specifically for auto dealer websites. It does three things:

1. **Helps shoppers find the right vehicle** by understanding natural language queries and searching your live inventory in real time.

2. **Answers questions about specific vehicles** using your actual listing data, so shoppers get accurate, VDP-level responses instantly.

3. **Qualifies and routes leads automatically** based on shopping behavior, so your sales team talks to buyers who are ready to move forward.

It works 24/7. It handles hundreds of conversations simultaneously. And every interaction captures data your team can use.

## How Nobi Works for Shoppers

The experience starts the moment a shopper lands on your website. Instead of navigating a maze of filters and menus, they see a search interface that accepts plain English.

### Natural Language Inventory Search

Shoppers type what they're actually looking for. Nobi understands context, lifestyle needs, and vehicle attributes, then searches your live inventory to surface matching results.

Here are real examples of the kinds of queries Nobi handles:

**Broad lifestyle search:**
> "I need an SUV for a family of 5 with good gas mileage under $40k"

Nobi interprets this as: third-row seating or 5+ passenger capacity, strong fuel economy ratings, SUV body style, and a price cap of $40,000. It returns matching vehicles from your current inventory with the details that matter most to this shopper.

**Specific feature search:**
> "Do you have any trucks with a tow package and under 30k miles?"

Nobi searches your inventory for trucks, filters by tow package availability and mileage, and presents the results. If you have 12 trucks that match, the shopper sees all 12 with the relevant specs highlighted.

**Comparison request:**
> "How does the Highlander compare to the Pilot you have in stock?"

Nobi pulls the listing data for both vehicles and presents a side-by-side comparison: price, mileage, features, fuel economy, and any other differentiators. The shopper gets the information they need to make a decision without opening six browser tabs.

**Budget-conscious search:**
> "What's the cheapest reliable sedan you have with a backup camera?"

Nobi understands that "cheapest" means sort by price ascending, "reliable" maps to brands and models with strong reliability ratings, and "backup camera" is a specific feature filter. It returns results accordingly.

### VDP-Level Answers

A huge share of shoppers land directly on a Vehicle Detail Page from a marketplace listing on CarGurus, AutoTrader, or Cars.com. They're already interested in a specific car. They have questions.

Nobi answers those questions instantly from your listing data:

> **Shopper:** "Does this Tahoe have a sunroof?"
> **Nobi:** "Yes, this 2024 Chevrolet Tahoe LT includes a power sunroof. It also has heated front seats, a Bose premium sound system, and a tow package."

> **Shopper:** "What's the warranty situation on this one?"
> **Nobi:** "This vehicle has 18,200 miles on it. The original factory powertrain warranty covers 60,000 miles, so you'd have approximately 41,800 miles of powertrain coverage remaining."

> **Shopper:** "Can I see this car in a different color?"
> **Nobi:** "This Tahoe is in Black. I found two other Tahoe LTs currently in stock: one in Summit White and one in Empire Beige Metallic. Want me to show you those?"

Every answer comes from your actual inventory data. Nobi doesn't guess. If the data isn't available for a specific question, it says so and offers to connect the shopper with your team.

### After-Hours Engagement

Your dealership closes at 8pm. Your shoppers don't. A significant portion of online vehicle research happens on evenings and weekends, exactly when nobody is available to answer questions.

Nobi doesn't have business hours. A shopper browsing your inventory at 11pm on a Wednesday gets the same quality experience as someone visiting at 2pm on a Tuesday. They can search inventory, ask questions about specific vehicles, compare options, and signal their intent, all without waiting until the next business day.

**That means leads are captured, qualified, and waiting for your team when they arrive in the morning.**

## How Nobi Works for Dealers

The shopper-facing experience is only half of what Nobi does. On the dealer side, every conversation generates actionable data.

### Automatic Lead Qualification

Not every shopper who asks a question is ready to buy. Some are casually browsing. Others are comparing your inventory against three other dealerships and ready to schedule a test drive this weekend.

**Nobi tells the difference.**

Throughout each shopping session, Nobi tracks behavioral signals that indicate buying intent:

- **Vehicle focus:** Is the shopper looking at one specific vehicle repeatedly, or browsing broadly?
- **Question depth:** Are they asking about colors and features, or about pricing, financing, and availability?
- **Comparison behavior:** Are they comparing two or three specific vehicles? That's a strong signal they're narrowing down.
- **Session engagement:** How long are they interacting? How many questions are they asking?
- **Return visits:** Has this shopper been back to your site multiple times this week?

These signals build a lead score in real time. When a shopper's behavior indicates high intent, Nobi flags them as a qualified lead.

### Real-Time Lead Routing

When a shopper hits the qualification threshold, Nobi doesn't just log a lead in a spreadsheet. It routes the lead to your sales team in real time, with full context.

Your rep sees exactly what the shopper has been looking at, what questions they asked, which vehicles they compared, and what their priorities are. Instead of calling a cold lead and saying "I see you submitted a form on our website," your rep can say, "I see you've been looking at the Tahoe LT and the Pilot. Want me to set up test drives for both this Saturday?"

**That's a different conversation.** It's warmer. It's more relevant. And it converts at a higher rate.

### Lead Context and Reporting

Every Nobi interaction generates a record your team can use:

- **Vehicles viewed and favorited** by each shopper
- **Questions asked** and answers received
- **Search queries** that reveal what the shopper cares about
- **Lead scores** based on behavioral signals
- **Time and date stamps** showing when shoppers are most active on your site

This data feeds into a dashboard your team can access. It shows which vehicles are generating the most interest, what questions shoppers ask most frequently, where shoppers drop off, and how your inventory matches what people are actually searching for.

**That's not just lead data. It's merchandising intelligence.** If 40 shoppers this month searched for hybrid SUVs under $35k and you only have two in stock, that tells you something about what to acquire next.

## Integration and Setup

Dealers have enough software to manage. Nobi is designed to fit into what you already have, not replace it.

### How It Connects to Your Inventory

Nobi integrates with your existing inventory feed, whether that comes from your DMS, a feed provider like HomeNet or vAuto, or your website platform's native inventory system. Once connected, Nobi pulls your live inventory data and keeps it in sync. When a vehicle sells, it disappears from Nobi's results. When a new unit hits the lot, it's searchable immediately.

There's no manual data entry. No CSV uploads. No weekly syncs that leave your assistant recommending cars that sold three days ago.

### How It Installs on Your Website

Nobi installs as a lightweight script on your existing dealer website. It works with the major dealer website platforms. Your web provider doesn't need to rebuild anything. In most cases, installation is a single line of code added to your site's header.

**Most dealers go from signup to live in less than a week.**

### No Disruption to Your Current Workflow

Nobi doesn't ask you to change how your team works. Leads route to your existing CRM or lead management process. Your BDC keeps doing what they do, they just get better leads with more context. Your sales team keeps selling, they just spend less time answering "Does it have leather seats?" for the hundredth time.
